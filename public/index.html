How a 10-minute expedition works

One expedition a day, then the screen goes away. No ads, no in-app purchases, no endless feed.

Open a Room

You get a 4-digit code and a QR code. Your kid scans it with their own tablet or phone, even from Grandma’s house in another state.

Solve Three Locks

A short story sets the scene. Each lock gives the Scout the objects and the Keeper the rules. Talking is allowed. Showing your screen is not.

Talk Off-Screen

The chest opens with one question to chat about at dinner. Your kid can try a solo chest, and the report shows what they did, where it got tricky, and what the next mission practices.

Three locks, three kinds of thinking

Every puzzle is built by a generator and checked by a solver, so it always has exactly one answer, and neither player can find it alone.
